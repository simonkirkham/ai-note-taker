Lens A — Frictionless single surface
One outliner where agenda and notes are the same list: a [] line is a topic, indented lines are its notes, Ctrl+Enter ticks & collapses it.
▶
Try this: Click into the Hiring backfill line, press Enter + Tab to add nested notes, then Ctrl+Enter to tick it — it collapses, timestamps, and the cursor jumps to the next topic. [ on an empty line makes a new topic.

How it serves the 4 needs
Pre-recordAgenda is just five [] topic lines, entered before the meeting — type [ on a blank line to add another.
Mark-offCtrl+Enter flips []→[x], dims + collapses the block, stamps a gutter timestamp.
Logical structureIndented lines auto-nest under the nearest topic, so the doc self-organises into topic → notes with no headings to manage.
Low frictionOne surface, keyboard-first; ticking auto-advances the cursor to the next un-ticked topic — you never jump around or lose your place.

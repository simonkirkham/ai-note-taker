Topics vs Headers — 9 design prototypes

Exploring how to record meeting topics ahead of time, tick them off as they come up, and still end with logically-structured notes — without jumping around.

“How to record topics ahead of time which need discussing, mark them off in conversation as they come up but also allow notes to be structured in a way which is logical based upon the conversation. The user shouldn't have to jump around too much to do this.”

Each prototype uses the same sample meeting (Weekly Team Sync, agenda discussed out of order — Hiring before Budget) so you compare the interaction, not the styling. Open each, try the key gesture, then come back.

★★ Round 3 — the build to evaluate: Gutter Dot × Markdown

Gutter Dot, as a block attribute that coexists with real markdown (### headers, bold, bullets)

BGutter Dot × Markdown — topic = an attribute on any block, not a line type

A ### header can be a topic or just a header; a paragraph or bullet can be a topic too. Ticking strikes in place — the note's markdown structure never changes. A live “view markdown” panel shows the round-trip (~~done~~ + an invisible <!--topic-->).
Try: hover a line → click its gutter dot to make it a topic, click again to tick discussed (in place). Dot the “rename the staging env” bullet (a non-header topic). Hit ⌁ Markdown to see it stay clean markdown.
  • Works with ### headers: topic/done are attributes, so a header stays a header (unlike checkbox lists, which can't also be headings)
  • Minimal structure change: nothing reflows; same single surface you type in

Round 1 — the original 9 (3 lenses × 3 ideas):

Lens A — Frictionless single surface

agenda & notes are one text stream; keyboard-first; no panels

1Checkline

Every line can become a tickable topic by starting it with []; notes you type below auto-nest under it.
Try: click Hiring, Enter+Tab to add nested notes, then Ctrl+Enter to tick & collapse — or hit “Auto-play”.
  • Pre-record: dump [] topic lines
  • Mark-off: Ctrl+Enter flips []→[x], collapses, timestamps
  • Structure: indented lines nest as the topic's notes
  • Low friction: cursor never leaves the text stream
Trade-off: the [] syntax is a learned convention.
Open prototype →

2Slash-Topic + Fold

Summon foldable topic headers with /topic; ticking a topic folds its body out of the way.
Try: Ctrl+. (or click the ◯) on a topic = mark discussed → folds to a “▸ N notes” summary & jumps to the next.
  • Pre-record: /topic Name drops a foldable header
  • Mark-off: ◯→● + auto-fold body
  • Structure: each header owns the lines beneath it; reorder carries the body
  • Low friction: slash + toggle + fold, all keystrokes
Trade-off: fold state hides notes — needs clear “N notes” affordance.
Open prototype →

3BuJo Migrate

Bullet-journal style: pre-write • topics, then “migrate” each down into the log as it's actually raised.
Try: focus a pool bullet, press Ctrl+↓ (or “↓ migrate”) — it flies down into spoken order; click a glyph to cycle •→✓→→→✕.
  • Pre-record: flat list of • topic bullets
  • Mark-off: glyph cycle (✓ done, → carried, ✕ dropped)
  • Structure: agenda drains into conversation order with notes beneath
  • Low friction: one key migrates; one click sets status
Trade-off: final note no longer matches the planned order.

Lens B — Agenda as a control panel

steer from a persistent, glanceable agenda beside the notes

4Cockpit Rail

Left rail = agenda checklist; a “now discussing →” pointer auto-files everything you type under the active topic.
Try: click Hiring (or press its number key) — pointer + coloured spine move there; type, and notes file themselves. Toggle Live/Grouped.
  • Pre-record: type topics into the left rail
  • Mark-off: checkbox = discussed + timestamp
  • Structure: notes auto-section in the order topics were raised
  • Low friction: one peripheral click; never leave the notes pane
Trade-off: forget to switch topics → notes pile under the wrong one.
Open prototype →

5Run-of-Show Strip

A top strip of topic chips doubles as a coverage meter; notes stream flat, a toggle re-flows them by topic.
Try: tap a chip (active→done), type into the flat stream, then flip “Group by topic” to watch it reflow into sections.
  • Pre-record: add chips L→R; drag to set running order
  • Mark-off: tap = active, tap again = done; progress “3/6”
  • Structure: deferred — “Group by topic” reflows on demand
  • Low friction: active chip persists; tap only at transitions
Trade-off: strip crowds past ~6–7 topics; structure isn't live.
Open prototype →

6Split-Pane Facilitator

Side-by-side: check a topic on the left → its section expands + focuses on the right; drag stray notes to re-file.
Try: check a topic on the left (it expands + focuses on the right, others collapse); drag the “Unsorted” line onto a topic.
  • Pre-record: left-pane topic accordions
  • Mark-off: checkbox = done (dim + ✓)
  • Structure: notes live literally inside their topic's body
  • Low friction: check does expand+focus for you; drag to correct
Trade-off: one section at a time; drag-refile is mouse-heavy.
Open prototype →

Lens C — Conversation-led reflow

capture chronologically; reorganise by topic with little/no effort

7Tickertape

Type in one chronological stream + one-tap a topic chip per line; a live right rail mirrors it grouped by topic.
Try: type a line, tap a topic chip under the input — it gets a colour border AND flies into its group on the right. Or “▶ Play meeting script”.
  • Pre-record: agenda chips in the left rail
  • Mark-off: first line filed → chip marks topic discussed
  • Structure: right rail assembles by-topic live; middle stays chronological
  • Low friction: last chip pre-selects; hands stay by the input
Trade-off: a tap per line — light but constant.
Open prototype →

8Listen & Suggest

The live transcript guesses the current topic — “Now discussing: X?” — and one tap confirms & back-files recent lines.
Try: hit ▶ Play, then ✓/✕ the mic pills as they appear (one suggestion is wrong — dismiss it).
  • Pre-record: agenda cards seed match keywords
  • Mark-off: confirm a suggestion → topic ticked
  • Structure: confirming back-files lines into the auto-outline
  • Low friction: only gesture is accept/dismiss a pill
Trade-off: quality hinges on transcript-match accuracy.
Open prototype →

9Reflow Scrubber

Capture a pure flat stream during the call; one “Reflow” pull auto-groups it by topic with a scrubbable timeline.
Try: pull “↡ Reflow by topic” — lines animate into groups, agenda auto-ticks; drag the ⚠ mis-filed line to fix it; drag the scrubber.
  • Pre-record: agenda checklist (reference only)
  • Mark-off: deferred — auto-ticks on reflow
  • Structure: one pull clusters chronological notes by topic
  • Low friction: zero in-call interaction; one gesture at the end
Trade-off: no live sense of what's covered until the end.

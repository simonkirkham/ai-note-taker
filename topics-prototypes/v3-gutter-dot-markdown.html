Gutter Dot × Markdown

A topic is a dot you add to any line — a ### header, a paragraph, or a bullet. It never changes the line's formatting; ticking just strikes it through in place.

Try: hover a line → click its gutter dot to make it a topic, click again to tick it discussed. “Agenda” & “Parking lot” are headers with no dot. Dot the “rename the staging env” bullet to make a non-header topic.

    Why this works with other markdown (### headers, bold, lists)
    

      
Topic = attributeTopic/done are flags layered on a block, not a line type — so a ### header can be a topic or just a header, and a bullet/paragraph can be a topic too.

      
No restructureMarking or ticking never converts the block or reflows the note; the strike happens in place.

      
Clean round-tripStored as ordinary markdown: ### ~~Budget~~ <!--topic-->. Portable; the marker is invisible when rendered.

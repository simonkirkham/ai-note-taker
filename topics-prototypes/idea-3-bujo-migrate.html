How it serves the 4 needs
  • Pre-record: the 5 agenda topics sit in the pool before the meeting — drafted ahead, not invented live.
  • Mark-off: one click cycles a topic's glyph • → ✓ → → → ✕ — ticking it off is a single gesture.
  • Logical structure: migrating drains the agenda into the log, so notes read in true conversation order with topic headers and ghosts of the original plan.
  • Low friction: Ctrl/Cmd + ↓ migrates the focused bullet to the cursor and drops you straight into typing — no menus, no drag.

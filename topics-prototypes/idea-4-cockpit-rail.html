How it serves the 4 needs
1 · Pre-record: the 5 topics live in the rail before the meeting; add more by typing + Enter.
2 · Mark off: tick a topic's checkbox → strikethrough + timestamp, no leaving the notes.
3 · Logically structured: every note inherits the active topic, so the record auto-sections by topic (Grouped view) regardless of typing order.
4 · Low-friction move: one peripheral click / number-key switches topic + moves the green pointer and coloured spine — you never leave the editor.

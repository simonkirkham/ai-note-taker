How it serves the 4 needs
  • Pre-record: the agenda checklist is loaded up front as reference — visible but never interactive during the call.
  • Mark off: deferred & automatic — agenda boxes tick themselves on reflow for every topic that got notes (AOB had none → stays unticked).
  • Logically structured: the flat stream reflows under inferred topic headings in one gesture — captured order, organised view.
  • Low-friction move: drag any line between headings to correct the inferred topic; nothing else to touch.

Variation on "Checkline" — single surface, type-first
Same one editable stream of [] topics + notes — but as you scroll, the topic you're in stays pinned to the top with its ✓, and a corner pill tracks coverage. Context without a side panel.

How it serves the 4 needs
Pre-recordAgenda is five [] topic lines typed before the meeting — [ on a blank line starts another.
Mark-offTick from the topic's own line or the pinned sticky header — soft strike + timestamp, and the coverage pill increments.
Logical structureNotes nest under the topic above them; the sticky header always names which topic the notes you're reading belong to.
Low frictionOne scrolling surface; the current topic + its ✓ follow you, and the corner pill flags what's still un-covered (AOB) without a panel.

How it serves the 4 needs
Pre-record: the 5 agenda topics are seeded as chips before the meeting — left rail + file-chip bar.
Mark off: the first line filed to a topic flips its left-rail chip grey→solid and stamps it "discussed".
Logically structured: the right rail re-stacks every line under its topic, so out-of-order capture reads as a clean by-topic record.
Low-friction move: one tap on a chip files a line — hands stay on the input + chip-bar; the last chip pre-selects for runs of same-topic lines.

Lens A — Frictionless single surface
One document. Type /topic to summon a foldable topic header; mark it done to auto-fold and jump to the next.
▶ Try this
1. Click a note line under Hiring, type a note, hit Enter. Then Ctrl+. (or click the ◯) to mark discussed — it folds and scrolls to the next ◯.
2. On a fresh line type / then topic Extra item + Enter to add a new foldable topic. Use the ⠿ handle (or Alt+↑/↓) to move a topic — its notes follow.

How it serves the 4 needs
Pre-recordThe 5 agenda topics are typed up front as /topic headers (each a ◯), building the agenda before the meeting starts.
Mark-offCtrl+. or clicking the ◯ flips it to ● and folds the body to a one-line summary, then scrolls the next undiscussed ◯ to view.
Logical structureEach header owns the lines beneath it; reordering a header (drag handle / Alt+arrows) carries its whole note block, so structure follows the jumpy conversation.
Low frictionOne keyboard-first surface — no panes, no modes. / summons a topic, Enter adds notes, Ctrl+. closes one out. Folded topics show "▸ N notes" so nothing feels lost.

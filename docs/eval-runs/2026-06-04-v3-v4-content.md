## Prompt summary

V4 moved **mean Content +0.028** (0.714→0.742) — its goal — but **mean Quality −0.005** (flat-to-down), because the strong-model regressions cancel the weak-model gains. Three things V4 did:

1. **Lifted content on the weaker models** — Nova Lite +0.100, Sonnet +0.048. On the **production model this is a real win** and the whole point of MPI-1.
2. **Regressed the strong models** — Mistral −0.054 Quality (and −0.116 Decisions), Opus −0.015. The depth instruction over-fires on models that were already thorough.
3. **Dropped Tags on all four** (−0.01 to −0.05) and **worsened fabrication on thin transcripts** — on `17-budget-review` and `14-all-hands-reorg`, V4 lost content on 7 of 8 cells, with the judge naming invented detail ("Cyberdyne", "Stark Industries") *more* than under V3. The grounding clamp in V4 lost to the depth push. **Faithfulness did not catch any of it** (scored ~1.0), confirming it can't gate grounding.

**Which prompt to keep:** `analysis@v3` stays the shipped prompt for now. **V4 is not shipped** — it fails MPI-1's no-new-fabrication bar and regresses tags everywhere, despite winning content on the production model.

**What the next iteration (`analysis@v5`) should target:** keep V4's depth wording (it earns Nova Lite +0.10 content on substantive meetings), but (a) add a hard "thin transcript → short note, never name an entity not in the source" clamp that actually beats the depth push, and (b) restore V3's tag guidance.
